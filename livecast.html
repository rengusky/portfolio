Livecast

Stay present in your presentation—even when you are not in the room.

Livecast lets you record an explanation and attach it to a presentation or an individual slide.

Feature overview
Media pending

It started with one question.

What if a presentation could carry the presenter’s explanation with it?

Record it. Make it yours. Place it.

  1. 01 Record Capture your explanation without leaving Show.
  2. 02 Style Choose a shape and background that fits the slide.
  3. 03 Attach Add it to the full presentation or only where context is needed.
Recording flow
Media pending

The feature belongs where people expect media.

Livecast sits inside Insert → Media. It feels like part of making a presentation, not a separate recording tool.
